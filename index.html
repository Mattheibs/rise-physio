About the practice

Rehab-focused care,built around you

RISE Physio is a rehab-focused physiotherapy practice at Rise Studios in Bergvliet. I help you recover from injury and surgery, rebuild your strength and get back to the things you love, with treatment built around you and steady progress that lasts.

Areas of care

Three areas of care,one calm space

Not sure which to book? If something feels more like an injury than tightness, start with a physio assessment.

01

Physiotherapy

Recover from injury or surgery, manage pain and move with confidence again.

Every first session starts with a thorough assessment to understand what’s driving your symptoms, followed by hands-on treatment and a clear, progressive rehab plan.

  • Sports injuries
  • Running injuries and running assessments
  • Neck and back pain
  • Jaw pain and TMJ disorders
  • Shoulder, hip, knee, ankle and foot pain
  • Muscle and tendon injuries
  • Post-operative rehabilitation
  • Chronic and persistent pain
  • Dry needling and acupuncture, where helpful
Book physio
02

Women’s health

Private, unhurried care through pregnancy, postnatal recovery and menopause.

Pelvic health concerns are common, and they’re treatable. Sessions are private and unhurried, and where appropriate include an internal pelvic floor assessment, which is always explained fully and only done with your consent.

  • Bladder leakage and urgency
  • Pelvic floor weakness
  • Pelvic organ prolapse
  • Pelvic pain and pelvic girdle pain
  • Pregnancy-related back and pelvic pain
  • Postnatal recovery and return to exercise
  • Abdominal separation
  • Pelvic floor changes through menopause
Book women’s health
03

Pilates and recovery

1:1 physio-led Pilates and sports massage to build strength and recover well.

Personalised 1:1 Pilates, built around your body and goals. Book it any time to build strength, control and mobility, or as the next step once your rehab is complete. Sports massage helps ease muscle tightness and supports recovery from training and events.

  • Physio-led Pilates, 1:1
  • Sports massage
  • Ongoing support alongside your Rise training

About Alex

Hi, I’mAlex Heiberg

I’m an HPCSA-registered physiotherapist with a special interest in musculoskeletal and orthopaedic rehab, sports and running injuries, and women’s health. My approach is hands-on, rehab-focused and evidence-based.

As a Type 1 diabetic, I know first-hand how much staying strong and active matters. Outside of work, you’ll find me swimming, running, or with family and friends.

I graduated summa cum laude from the University of the Western Cape and started at a rural hospital in KwaZulu-Natal, before working in a cardiac ICU in Cape Town.

Three years in London is where rehab became the heart of my work: first as a first-contact physio across four clinics with pitchside cover for school rugby, then at Six Physio, alongside leading sports medicine and orthopaedic consultants. There I used objective strength testing to guide patients back to sport, ran physio-led reformer Pilates and trained new rehab physios.

Back in Cape Town, I spent a year and a half in private practice alongside orthopaedic specialists, treating a high volume of foot and ankle injuries. I also offer running assessments and specialised running rehab.

I’ve completed a women’s health foundations course focusing on pelvic health, and trained in acupuncture, dry needling and reformer Pilates.
